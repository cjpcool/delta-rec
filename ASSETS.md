The supplied `deltarec_weights.zip` and `deltarec_frozen_assets.zip` archives, listed in `experiments.json`, must be extracted alongside this directory so their `deltarec/checkpoints/` and `deltarec/data/` merge into it. Obtain ML-20M from https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/20m/ and unpack `ratings.csv` into `data/raw/ml-20m/`. Its terms prohibit redistribution without separate permission: https://files.grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ml-20m-README.html . Obtain the 2014 Amazon Books **ratings-only** `ratings_Books.csv` (22,507,155 rows) from https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~jmcauley/datasets/amazon/links.html ; the 5-core download and newer editions are different inputs. Obtain `KuaiRand-1K.tar.gz` from https://zenodo.org/records/10439422 and unpack its `data/` into `data/raw/KuaiRand-1K/data/`.

Rating evaluations additionally require the original frozen `data/ml-20m/candidates/validation/` and `data/amazon-books/candidates/validation/` assets. These are full ordered top-100 retrieval results, exclude seen history, and do not force in the positive. Their histories, ID mapping and target coverage are bound to the corresponding dataset. They cannot be substituted with new negative sampling. Category groupings (`data/*/grouping.pt`, HSTU grouping assets) and checkpoint filenames are listed in configurations and `experiments.json`. Required frozen assets currently exist only in the working copy/local companion archive, not at a public anonymous URL.

Exported inference checkpoints contain all model, prediction-head, selector, frozen-embedding and grouping tensors they consume, with no original-module objects or parent paths. Training prefix/initializer tensors are separate explicit dependencies. HSTU ML-20M resumes a previously trained full-history initializer; its two short teacher continuations do not represent training from scratch. The selected LinRec configurations use native linear-attention KV interventions and grouped history selection, not the GDR core; the retained `full-gdr` stage name is the inherited CLI spelling. FuXi CWI in the selected implementation gates input embeddings; this differs from a full recurrent-transition intervention. HSTU Amazon validates selector G=2 and then uses sparse G=16. Shared selection rounding/recent rules differ across backends; the configurations and original operations retain those differences.

HSTU Amazon source checkpoint selection used a 1,024-request validation shard and was marked ineligible for a full-headline claim. The measured result supplied here uses all 576,890 validation requests; it remains below the paper target. Final ranker microbatches and supervision chunk are retained through the existing execution-override mechanism (`sparse_execution` in the configuration).
